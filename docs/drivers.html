How the drivers are calculated
  • Two periods are compared. The change is split across the members of the breakdown, and the parts add up to the change exactly.
  • Mix: the member's share of contacts changed. More contacts in a member that sits below average pulls the result down, even if nobody's performance changed.
  • Rate: the member's own result changed, weighted by its average share of contacts in the two periods.
  • Volume has no mix or rate, only each member's own change.
  • A driver is arithmetic, not proof of cause. It shows where the change is concentrated. A different breakdown can tell a different part of the story.
  • Small samples (below the metric's minimum in either period) are grouped as "Other (small samples)" in the waterfall, greyed in the table, and left out of the findings.
  • Site, team and agent apply to handle time, first contact resolution, CSAT and NPS, because abandoned contacts never reach an agent. Agents can be combined with queue and reason, but not with customer filters.
  • Surveys count in the month they were answered, as on the scorecard.
