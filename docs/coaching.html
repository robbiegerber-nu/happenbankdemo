Agent & team coaching

How to read this page
  • Agents are compared only with their own group (phone, chat or email). Handle time, resolution, transfers and satisfaction are compared with what the same queues and contact reasons took the whole group, so a harder mix of work doesn't count against anyone.
  • A gap is marked only when it is well beyond chance (3 standard errors) and there are enough contacts, surveys, days or evaluations. Everything else reads "similar".
  • A coaching focus is consistent when the same gap was also there in the 3 months before. Agents in their first 90 days are shown as ramping up and never listed. Those 3 months must also fall after the agent's first 90 days.
  • Short handle time is never marked as good: it can mean rushed contacts. Occupancy isn't shown because agents don't control it.
  • Agents and teams are listed by name, not ranked. Use this to start a conversation, not to judge performance on its own.
  • Agents appear under their current team in every month. An agent's quality always covers 3 months, because about four contacts a month are evaluated.
